    Find your fastest way to the single finish ring. Try low gaps, saddle crossings, climbs, and wide turns. Maverik follows its trench, ridge, valley, and escape objectives first. Stay below the race ceiling. Free flight has no ceiling.

    Steep climbs bleed speed, even on afterburner. If you stall, lower the nose and add power. Flaps deploy during a drift and at low speed; the other control surfaces follow your inputs.

    Free fly: G lowers the gear (fixed on the Cessna). Wait for it to lock. Land slowly on a runway or flat ground with level wings and a gentle descent; the HUD shows your speed limit. Space brakes after touchdown. Shift adds power; S lifts off.
